## Why This is Unquestionably the Best Todo App Ever Built

### 1. Dynamic Target Optimization™ (The Runaway Delete Button)
Why should deleting a task be boring? 
- Our proprietary `del-btn` uses kinetic evasion algorithms to dart away across the screen when your cursor approaches.
- **Physical Health Benefit:** Trains your micro-reflexes and fine motor hand-eye coordination.
- **Cognitive Fitness:** You must solve real-time arithmetic (`23 × 17 = ?`) to verify your intellectual worthiness before deletion.
- **Emotional Mindfulness:** An emotional checkpoint checks if you are emotionally prepared to abandon your note.
- **Grip Strength Training:** A mandatory 3-second unyielding mouse press ensures you truly care.
- **Surprise Mechanics:** 35% chance of deleting a completely different task to teach you non-attachment to worldly possessions.

### 2. Non-Deterministic Productivity (NDP)
Other apps guarantee that tasks stay in order. **How dull.**
- TodoNothing sorts your tasks in random order upon every render.
- Tasks randomly swap their words for `"quack"`, `"banana"`, or `"spaghetti"`.
- Text input fields feature anti-monotony filters: letters randomly capitalize, reverse direction, or substitute keystrokes if you type too quickly.

### 3. Ergonomic Boundary Enforcement (Evasive Checkboxes)
- Checkboxes possess free will. 60% of the time, they teleport away when hovered.
- 15% of the time, they go on mandatory union break and refuse to be checked.
- Because true productivity is respecting a checkbox's personal boundaries.

### 4. Floating Ergonomic Sidebar
- Never stretch your wrist to the same side of the screen again! Our sidebar continuously relocates itself across the viewport every 3.5 seconds to promote balanced wrist exertion.
- The list deletion slider actively slides backward every 50ms, ensuring list deletion is an athletic milestone.
